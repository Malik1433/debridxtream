## LAYOUT SPECIFICATIONS

### Container
- Padding: 24dp all sides
- Background: Dark charcoal gradient
- No scrolling - all content visible

### Header (60dp height)
- Logo: Left aligned, 28sp
- Icons: Right aligned, 36×36dp

### Navigation Buttons (70dp total height with margins)
- Size: 120×48dp each
- Spacing: 16dp between
- Total width: 4×120 + 3×16 = 528dp (centered)

- Header: 20sp, 12dp margin bottom
- Cards: 200×112dp (16:9 ratio, compact)
- Spacing: 12dp between cards

- Spacing: 12dp between cards

- Spacing: 12dp between cards

- Cards: 100×100dp square logos
- Spacing: 12dp between cards

### Favorites Section (190dp total)
- Header: 20sp, 12dp margin bottom
- Cards: 110×160dp with heart icon
- Spacing: 12dp between cards
