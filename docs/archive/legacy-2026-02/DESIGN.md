# Design System: DebridXtream TV (Luxury Gold)
**Project ID:** 12973714338562703236 (Adapted for Android TV)

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
**Luxurious, Elegant, & Exclusive.**
This theme channels a high-end cinema experience. It relies on deep, velvety blacks and rich charcoal greys to let the content shine, accented by sophisticated metallic Gold/Amber tones for a touch of exclusivity and warmth. It feels like a VIP lounge.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
*   **Velvet Black:** (`#0a0a0a`) - The primary background. Deeper than standard black, feeling rich and immersive.
*   **Charcoal Surface:** (`#1c1c1c`) - Used for cards and sidebars to distinguish them from the background without harsh contrast.
*   **Luxury Gold:** (`#D4AF37`) to **Amber** (`#F59E0B`) gradient - Used for primary actions (buttons), focus indicators, and active icons.
*   **Pearl White:** (`#ededed`) - Primary text. Slightly softened white for eye comfort.
*   **Metallic Silver:** (`#9ca3af`) - Secondary text and inactive icons.
*   **Focus Halo:** (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`) - A soft gold glow around focused elements.

## 3. Typography Rules
**Font Family:** Plus Jakarta Sans (Clean and Modern)
*   **Display/Hero:** Bold, 32sp+.
*   **Headings:** Semi-bold, 20sp-24sp.
*   **Body:** Regular, 16sp.
*   **Metadata:** Medium, 14sp (Silver).

## 4. Component Stylings
*   **Focus States (TV Critical):**
    *   **Scale:** 1.05x expansion.
    *   **Border:** thin Gold border (`1px solid #D4AF37`).
    *   **Glow:** Soft Gold halo effect.
*   **Buttons:**
    *   **Primary:** Gradient Gold (`linear-gradient(90deg, #D4AF37, #F59E0B)`) with dark text (`#000000`) for high contrast/readability.
    *   **Secondary:** Charcoal with Gold border.
*   **Cards:**
    *   **Shape:** 6px rounded corners (Refined, closer to classic cinema screens).
    *   **Background:** Charcoal (`#1c1c1c`).

## 5. Layout Principles
*   **Elegance:** High contrast between Gold and Black.
*   **Spacing:** Balanced.
*   **Navigation:** Minimalist left sidebar.
