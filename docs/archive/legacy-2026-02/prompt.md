# Enhanced Stitch Prompt: 3D Cinematic Home Screen Redesign

Create a **high-end, cinematic Android TV Home Screen** featuring a **3D "Top 10" Row** and distinct, immersive section designs. The interface must feel alive with depth and premium animations.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- **Platform:** Android TV (Landscape, 10-foot UI)
- **Theme:** Luxury Gold & Velvet Black (Dark Mode Only)
- **Background:** Deep Velvet Black (#0a0a0a) for infinite depth.
- **Primary Accent:** Luxury Gold Gradient (#D4AF37 to #F59E0B) for active elements and the "Top 10" numbers.
- **Surface:** Glassmorphic Charcoal (#1c1c1c with 90% opacity) for the sidebar.
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans (Bold headings, clean metadata).
- **Focus State:** **Critical.** Items must scale **1.1x** with a **3D tilt effect** and a **soft gold glow** (#D4AF37).

**Page Structure & Layout:**

1.  **Glassmorphic Sidebar (Left):**
    *   A floating, semi-transparent vertical bar.
    *   **Icons:** Minimalist white icons that glow Gold when focused.
    *   **Text:** Hidden by default, expands/fades in only when the sidebar acts as the main focus context.

2.  **Hero / Featured Carousel (Top):**
    *   Large, immersive background backdrop that changes with selection.
    *   Gradient overlay at the bottom to blend into the content rows.

3.  **"Top 10" Row (New Feature):**
    *   **Style:** A horizontal list of the top 10 movies/series.
    *   **Numbers:** Big, **3D Metallic Gold numbers (1-10)** placed prominently behind or overlapping the bottom-left of each poster. Usually roughly half the height of the poster.
    *   **Posters:** High-resolution vertical movie posters.
    *   **3D Effect:** On focus, the poster pops out (z-index) and tilts slightly to follow the user's perspective.

4.  **Categorized Content Rows:**
    *   Each row (e.g., "Trending Now", "New Releases") should have a **distinct layouts**:
        *   *Standard Row:* Rounded rectangular cards.
        *   *Wide Row:* 16:9 aspect ratio thumbnails for episodes/live TV.
    *   **Animation:** Smooth horizontal scrolling with a "magnetic" snap effect.

**Animation & 3D specifics:**
-   **Entrance:** Elements should cascade in with a subtle 3D fade-up.
-   **Focus:** "Breathe" animation—gentle pulse on the gold glow.
-   **Material:** Surfaces should appear to have thick, premium material (not flat paper), with realistic shadows.
