# DebridXtream TV - MASTER DESIGN SYSTEM
**Version:** 2.0 (Enhanced with UI/UX Pro Max Intelligence)

## 1. Visual Identity: Luxury Gold
**Atmosphere:** Premium, Cinematic, Exclusive.
**Primary Base:** Velvet Black (`#0a0a0a`)
**Secondary Base:** Charcoal Surface (`#1c1c1c`)
**Accents:** 
- **Luxury Gold:** `#D4AF37` (Primary Focus & Buttons)
- **Amber Glow:** `#F59E0B` (Gradients & Highlights)

## 2. Typography (Plus Jakarta Sans)
- **H1 (Hero):** 32sp / Bold / Pearl White
- **H2 (Section):** 24sp / Semi-Bold / Pearl White
- **Body:** 16sp / Regular / Pearl White (85% Opacity)
- **Metadata:** 14sp / Medium / Metallic Silver (`#9ca3af`)

## 3. Spacing & Grid (8px Base)
- **Container Padding:** 32px (Sides), 24px (Top/Bottom)
- **Card Gap:** 16px
- **Inter-Section Spacing:** 48px

## 4. Interaction Rules (TV Critical)
### Focus States
- **Scale:** 1.05x expansion with `250ms ease-out` transition.
- **Border:** `2dp` solid Luxury Gold (`#D4AF37`).
- **Glow:** `8dp` elevation shadow with `#D4AF37` tint.

### Performance & Loading
- **Skeleton Screens:** Use desaturated charcoal pulse for loading states.
- **Content Jumping:** Reserve space for movie posters to prevent layout shifts.

### Accessibility
- **Contrast:** Maintain 4.5:1 ratio for all metadata text.
- **Visible Navigator:** Sidebar must always show an active indicator (Gold vertical bar).

## 5. Components
### Cards (Movie/Series)
- **Radius:** 6px
- **Hover:** Show title and rating in a soft gold gradient overlay.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Gradient (`#D4AF37` to `#F59E0B`), internal text Black.
- **Secondary:** Transparent with `1dp` Gold border.
